Roguelike Gauntlet

How the Roguelike realm works, and every boon and curse you can draft.

Overview

Roguelike Gauntlet is the Classless realm built around dungeon challenges, not levelling. You make a Hero, pick the level you want to play at, and draft a build from the classes the server offers you: abilities, talents, glyphs and gear. Then you take on dungeons with the boons and curses you drafted.

  • Hero class: a Hero has no fixed class. It plays with whatever its draft gives it, and the Classless UI only shows a Hero the classes, spells and talent trees it drafted.
  • Server-owned randomness: every offer (classes, abilities, boon and curse rows) is rolled by the server and saved. Relogging shows you the same offers, so you can't reroll by reconnecting.
  • Cross-faction: Alliance and Horde can group, trade and run dungeons together.
  • Curses are about dungeons: most curses add hazards or make enemies stronger, much like Mythic+ affixes. Boons make you stronger in dungeons. There are no XP, gold or loot-economy picks.
  • The realm shares accounts with every other Classless realm. Pick Roguelike Gauntlet from the realm list.

Character creation

Create a Hero. When you first log in, the Gauntlet window opens. It's an 8-step wizard, and it keeps reopening until you lock in a build (/gauntlet opens it too).

  1. Classes: choose how many classes to draft (1–5, default 3). Each pick is a row of 3 classes, and you keep one per row. You get 3 re-rolls, shared with the ability draft.
  2. Level: pick any starting level that falls inside at least one dungeon's level range (the lowest is 15, Ragefire Chasm). The full list is below. The optional Start at picker on this page lists only the dungeons that fit the level you chose (default: none). If you pick one, you're teleported just outside its entrance about 1 second after you lock in (no teleporter cooldown); if you're dead, in combat or on a flight path it tells you to use /tp instead.
  3. Abilities: for each ability slot you're offered 3 whole spells from your drafted classes and keep 1 (pick 3, keep 1). You get every rank up to your level. Spells that come from talents are never offered (take the talent instead), and each class's core kit is free (see below). Besides “Re-roll all”, you get 2 single-row re-rolls per draft (the R button at the end of a row). It swaps in 3 new spells for that row only and keeps your other picks. Each spell appears at most once on the whole table, and a row re-roll never brings back a spell that's already shown or one you re-rolled away. If your classes don't have enough spells at your level, some cards say “No more spells” instead of repeating one.
  4. Talents: spend your talent points in the trees of your drafted classes. Classic tree rules apply: tier N needs N×5 points in that tree, and prerequisites must be maxed.
  5. Glyphs: choose glyphs from your drafted classes. Slots open with level like a normal character: 15 = 1 major + 1 minor, 30 = 2+1, 50 = 2+2, 70 = 2+3, 80 = 3+3.
  6. Gear: automatic gear for a role (strength, agility, intellect or tank) up to an item level you choose, plus class weapon kits in your bags. See Realm Rules.
  7. Boons & Curses: two independent sliders and one pick per row, plus optional Boss Rolls (a pick-1-of-3 draft after each dungeon boss) and Ramping (tougher enemies per dungeon clear) checkboxes, both off by default. See the draft.
  8. Review: check everything and lock in. The server checks the whole build again and applies it: level, spells, talents, gear, boons and curses, core kit and glyphs.

Realm rules

  • Rest replaces food and drink. Every character knows Rest. Cast it out of combat to sit down and regain 10% health and mana every second for 10 seconds. Standing up, moving or entering combat ends it. Water still matters for the drunk curses and boons (vendors sell it).
  • Hero mana regeneration is 50% above the normal baseline (1.5×).
  • Gear: automatic gear is never PvP gear (no resilience, no arena or honor items). Tanks get plate (mail below level 40) and a shield. Class kits go into your bags: Rogue 2 daggers; Warrior and Paladin a two-hander, a one-hander and a shield; Hunter a bow or gun; Shaman a two-hander and two different one-handers. Strength, agility and tank builds get a bow or gun, and intellect builds get a wand.
  • Ammo: every Hero gets 1000 arrows and 1000 bullets close to their level, plus a quiver and ammo pouch. Ammo is unlimited, so the stack never runs out.
  • Teleporter: click the teleporter button on the minimap (drag it around the edge to move it), or type /tp. The Dungeons page lists all 55 dungeon entrances with their level ranges (green means it fits your level, and the “For my level” tab filters to those), and it takes you just outside the door you pick. Cities, zones and raid entrances are there too. You have to be alive, out of combat, not in a dungeon, raid, battleground or arena, and not on a flight path. There's a 30-second cooldown.
  • Dungeon revive: if you die inside a dungeon or raid, click Release Spirit and you're brought back right away with 50% health and mana at the spot where you entered, with no ghost run. Until you release, a battle res, soulstone or the Second Chance boon works as usual.
  • Death Knight runes refill properly for Heroes, and Heroes get the combo point frame.
  • Tracker window: a small window you can drag lists your active boons, curses, pacts, trials and the dungeon affixes active where you are. Hover a line for details; -/+ collapses it. /gauntlet hide toggles it, /gauntlet reset ui moves it back, and /gauntlet opens the full list.
  • Fresh Build: want to start over? Press Fresh Build... in the Boons & Curses window or type /freshbuild, type RESET, then confirm the red Wipe now. This can't be undone by you: it deletes every item (worn, bags, bank, keyring, currency) and your gold, Gauntlet mail, your drafted spells, talents, glyphs, boons, curses, trials and Boss Rolls, and puts you back to level 1. You're logged out, and on your next login character creation starts again at step 1. Reputation, quests, achievements, professions, player mail and your group are kept. You must be alive, out of combat, not on a flight path and not in a dungeon, raid, battleground or arena (or a battleground queue). One reset per 10 minutes. Creation then runs as normal, including the Start at dungeon picker. The server saves a backup first, so a GM can restore a mistaken reset.
Mobile Casting needs the current launcher. The Mobile Casting boon lets you cast and channel while moving. The server allows it, but your game client also has to. The current Windows and Linux launchers (download them from the main page) build and start a patched Wow_cwm.exe next to Wow.exe. If you start the stock Wow.exe, the boon still works on the server, but your client stops the cast. On Linux and Steam, point the shortcut at the launcher (launch-classless.sh --sync, which now updates itself) or run steam/install-steam-shortcut.sh. The macOS launcher doesn't support it. A working install writes cwm.log saying “installed 4/4 hooks”.

Update Character & the next dungeon

Update Character

Once your build is locked you can level it up without starting over. Type /gauntlet update or press Update Character in the teleporter window. It works any time, even in the middle of a dungeon.

  • Add-only: your level only goes up, never down. Spells, talents, boons, curses and bags are never removed or re-granted.
  • Optional new item level: the window has a Gear toggle. Leave it on keep current (the default) and your gear isn't touched. Switch it to new item level and pick 1–300 (same range as creation) to get a fresh auto-picked set at that item level, equipped:
    • Gear the Gauntlet gave you is replaced (deleted).
    • Other worn items you earned are moved to your bags, or mailed to you if your bags are full.
    • Enchanted or gemmed items stay equipped.
    • With a curated gear set, the new item level is only saved.
    Running the same update twice does nothing extra.
  • Talent points: +1 per level gained. Spend them in the normal Classless talent window.
  • Abilities: your drafted abilities learn their new ranks for the new level. You also get 1 new ability pick for every 5 levels gained since you locked in (pick 1 of 3 from your drafted classes, never a spell you already have). The cards are rolled once and kept, so there's no free re-roll. Picks you don't take stay open for a later update.
  • Optional teleport: choose a dungeon that fits the new level and you're taken to its entrance (alive, out of combat and not on a flight path).
  • Checks: the server checks the update with the same rules as creation (the level must fit a dungeon, picks must be cards you were offered). A relog never undoes an update.

Final boss: what next?

When a dungeon's final boss dies, every Hero in the group inside the instance and within 100 yd gets a “what next?” window, shown after any Boss Roll window is closed. It comes up once per run, and kills by your pet count too.

  • Update spec: opens Update Character.
  • Same spec, new dungeon: +3 levels (or up to the chosen dungeon's minimum level, max 80), no new abilities, then teleports you to the dungeon you pick.
  • Stay here: closes the window.

It doesn't fire in The Culling of Stratholme (Mal'ganis never dies) or Halls of Reflection (the run ends with an escape, not a kill).

The Boons & Curses draft

Step 7 of creation has two independent sliders. Each one goes from 0 to 8, and each step on a slider adds one row of 3 server-rolled cards. You keep one card per row. All boons and no curses is allowed, so is all curses and no boons, and so is none of either.

Boons: 3

Curses: 2

  • Re-rolls: you get 1 free row re-roll, plus 1 more for every curse-row pick. Pacts, Trials and Dungeon Affixes come from curse rows, so they use the Curses slider and earn re-rolls.
  • Conflicts: two picks clash when they push the same thing in opposite directions (Swift vs Powerful, Speedy vs Leaden, Blessed vs Mortal Wounds). Clashing cards are dimmed. Picks that push the same way stack, up to a cap.
  • Damage floors: curse damage (Blood Curse, Living Bomb, Germaphobic) never takes you below 10% health, and affix damage never kills you. The Immortal Snail Problem is the one exception: it kills on contact.
  • Dungeon affixes are shared: a dungeon gets the affix curses of every Hero inside it, and each affix counts once even if two Heroes picked it. They apply to everyone in that instance, including other players and bots.
  • Area warnings: area curses and affixes draw a ring on the ground at their real radius: Volcanic 3 yd, Entangling 4 yd, Sanguine pools 5 yd, Quaking 8 yd, Living Bomb 10 yd.
  • Seasonal affixes: each dungeon affix is a Mythic+ affix, and its card shows the season it comes from. Fortified and Tyrannical are from Legion 7.0 and have been in every season since. The newest five (Sanguine, Explosive, Spiteful, Storming and Entangling) come from seasons between Legion and Dragonflight.
  • Visuals: curses show on your character (and affixes on enemies). Boons have no glow.
  • Boss Rolls (optional): tick the Boss Rolls box in step 7 and every dungeon boss you help kill opens a small draft: 3 cards, take 1, once per boss for that character (re-kills and resets don't roll again). Each card is a boon or a curse, and the odds follow your sliders: the chance of a boon is boons ÷ (boons + curses), so with 0 curses you only see boons and with 0 boons only curses; with both at 0 it's 50/50. No card is a duplicate or clashes with what you have. You can't skip a roll, but you can press Decide later: it shrinks to a pulsing Boss Roll button, several rolls queue up and show one at a time, and they wait across logouts. What you pick is announced with a raid warning and a chat line and shows up in your Boons & Curses window right away. Off unless you tick it.
  • Ramping (optional, harder): tick the Ramping box in step 7 and every 5-player dungeon you clear (its final boss dies while you're inside) makes the next ones tougher: enemies deal +5% damage per clear, added up (7 clears = +35%), and take less damage on a curve that levels off and never goes past 60% damage reduction: 1 clear 5%, 5 clears 20%, 10 clears 34%, 20 clears 49%, 40 clears 58%. (With Fortified or Tyrannical on top, enemies take at most about 69% less damage.) Each run counts once per character. In a group, the dungeon uses the highest clear count among the ramping Heroes inside. It's set when you enter, goes up if someone with more clears joins, and never goes down during the run (a clear you earn there counts from your next dungeon). It applies to every enemy in that dungeon, bosses and affix spawns included, for everyone in it, and stacks with Fortified and Tyrannical. Raids, battlegrounds and the open world are never ramped. Mal'ganis (Culling of Stratholme) doesn't die, so that dungeon doesn't count. Your window shows e.g. “Ramping: 7 clears, +35% enemy damage, 27% damage reduction”. Off unless you tick it, and a Fresh Build resets the count.

Chaos events

Four curses and five boons are chaos events: things that happen to your party in the middle of a dungeon fight. They're drafted like any other card and show up in the Boons and Curses lists below.

The shared timer

  • Which events: Madness, Hollowed Ground, Portals, Moonwell, Vanish and Call in the Artillery use this timer. Hydra applies to every fight, and The Light and Reincarnation trigger on low health and death.
  • Party-wide: your group inside one dungeon or raid shares one timer, and it can fire any chaos event that any Hero in the group drafted. Solo, you're a party of one. Never outside dungeons and raids.
  • Combat seconds only: it only counts while someone in the party is in combat.
  • It ramps: the chance starts at zero and rises every combat second. About half of events have fired by 1:30 of combat, and one always fires by 3:00.
  • One at a time: the timer stops while an event runs, and every event runs to the end even if combat drops.
  • 1-minute grace after each event ends, then a fresh window starts from zero.
  • Everything chaos events spawn gives no loot, XP or kill credit, and isn't buffed by affixes.

Curses

CurseWhat happens
MadnessA random party member (bots too) is mind controlled and attacks the party at full damage. There's no set duration: it lasts until they've dealt damage equal to 100% of their own max health to teammates, they die, or the whole party is out of combat. Crowd control and dispels don't break it. (A hidden 60-second failsafe only exists to stop it getting stuck.) Alone, you lose control (fear) for 5 seconds instead.
Hollowed GroundA restless spirit rises 6–12 yd from a party member at 15% health. Heal it to full within 20 seconds and it's laid to rest. If not, the grave bursts and 3 zombies (+1 per extra party member) climb out and fight for up to 90 seconds.
PortalsHalf the party (rounded down, at least 1) is pulled into the shadow realm: the same spot in the same dungeon, phased out of sight of everyone else. Each split player faces 1–3 of that dungeon's regular trash mobs (never bosses) at the dungeon's difficulty. When they're all dead a portal appears; use it to go back to the exact spot you left. Stay within 30 yd or you're pulled back to your spot. If the main party wipes meanwhile, the split players keep fighting and come back to their wiped party. If you die in the shadow realm you're returned at once and the normal revive rules apply. After 5 minutes everyone is sent back. Never happens solo. No reward.
HydraEvery fight in a dungeon is a Hydra fight (no timer, not on the shared timer). As soon as combat starts, every non-boss mob fighting you becomes part of the Hydra pack, and mobs that join the fight later are added too. Nothing rises until the first pack mob dies: 15 seconds after that first death, and every 15 seconds after, each living pack mob raises up to 2 of the pack's dead (oldest deaths first) where they fell, so 1 survivor brings back 2 and 3 survivors can bring back 6. Each time the same mob rises it has 20% less maximum health: 80%, then 60%, 40% and 20%; after its 4th rise it stays dead (a raised copy counts its original's rises, and the lost health can't be healed back). You get a center-screen warning and a chat line 3 seconds before each wave: “The Hydra stirs: N fallen will rise in 3 seconds!”. Kill them close together. Raised copies drop no loot and give no XP (the original corpse keeps its loot). Bosses and boss adds never rise: on a boss pull only the regular trash in that fight is affected. Only the main party's fight counts, not the shadow realm. It ends when the whole pack is dead, or the party is out of combat for 10 seconds.

Boons

BoonWhat happens
MoonwellA Moonwell springs up at a Hero's feet: for 12 seconds everyone within 8 yd regains 6% health and 6% mana every second.
VanishYou vanish (threat and combat dropped, stealthed) and turn invisible for 6 seconds. Your next ability, spell hit or heal within 20 seconds does 150% (not auto-attacks or damage-over-time ticks).
ReincarnationOnce per dungeon run, when you die you rise on the spot half a second later with 30% health and mana (no reagent, no sickness). It comes before Second Chance, which stays unspent, and before you'd need to release for the Dungeon revive. It isn't on the timer.
Call in the Artillery4 volleys, 3 seconds apart. Each volley marks 4 fire rings (5 yd) on hostile mobs within 40 yd, in combat or not; 2 seconds later the shells land for 12% of each enemy's max health (3% on bosses). Watch out, it can pull idle packs. Never hits friendly or neutral units, critters or pets.
The Light Shines on the WorthyWhen a party member in combat drops below 35% health, a light appears beside them. Heal it to full within 15 seconds and it becomes an Avatar of Light that fights for the party for up to 60 seconds, losing 4% health per second unless you keep healing it. At most one light per minute (1-minute cooldown after it ends).
