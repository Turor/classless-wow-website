Roguelike Gauntlet

How the Roguelike realm works, and every boon and curse you can draft.

Overview

Roguelike Gauntlet is the Classless realm built around dungeon challenges, not levelling. You make a Hero, pick the level you want to play at, and draft a build from the classes the server offers you: abilities, talents, glyphs and gear. Then you take on dungeons with the boons and curses you drafted.

  • Hero class: a Hero has no fixed class. It plays with whatever its draft gives it, and the Classless UI only shows a Hero the classes, spells and talent trees it drafted.
  • Server-owned randomness: every offer (classes, abilities, boon and curse rows) is rolled by the server and saved. Relogging shows you the same offers, so you can't reroll by reconnecting.
  • Cross-faction: Alliance and Horde can group, trade and run dungeons together.
  • Curses are about dungeons: most curses add hazards or make enemies stronger, much like Mythic+ affixes. Boons make you stronger in dungeons. There are no XP, gold or loot-economy picks.
  • The realm shares accounts with every other Classless realm. Pick Roguelike Gauntlet from the realm list.

Character creation

Create a Hero. When you first log in, the Gauntlet window opens. It's an 8-step wizard, and it keeps reopening until you lock in a build (/gauntlet opens it too).

  1. Classes: choose how many classes to draft (1–5, default 3). Each pick is a row of 3 classes, and you keep one per row. You get 3 re-rolls, shared with the ability draft.
  2. Level: pick any starting level that falls inside at least one dungeon's level range (the lowest is 15, Ragefire Chasm). The full list is below.
  3. Abilities: for each ability slot you're offered 3 whole spells from your drafted classes and keep 1 (pick 3, keep 1). You get every rank up to your level. Spells that come from talents are never offered (take the talent instead), and each class's core kit is free (see below). Besides “Re-roll all”, you get 2 single-row re-rolls per draft (the R button at the end of a row). It swaps in 3 new spells for that row only and keeps your other picks.
  4. Talents: spend your talent points in the trees of your drafted classes. Classic tree rules apply: tier N needs N×5 points in that tree, and prerequisites must be maxed.
  5. Glyphs: choose glyphs from your drafted classes. Slots open with level like a normal character: 15 = 1 major + 1 minor, 30 = 2+1, 50 = 2+2, 70 = 2+3, 80 = 3+3.
  6. Gear: automatic gear for a role (strength, agility, intellect or tank) up to an item level you choose, plus class weapon kits in your bags. See Realm Rules.
  7. Boons & Curses: two independent sliders and one pick per row. See the draft.
  8. Review: check everything and lock in. The server checks the whole build again and applies it: level, spells, talents, gear, boons and curses, core kit and glyphs.

Realm rules

  • Rest replaces food and drink. Every character knows Rest. Cast it out of combat to sit down and regain 10% health and mana every second for 10 seconds. Standing up, moving or entering combat ends it. Water still matters for the drunk curses and boons (vendors sell it).
  • Hero mana regeneration is 50% above the normal baseline (1.5×).
  • Gear: automatic gear is never PvP gear (no resilience, no arena or honor items). Tanks get plate (mail below level 40) and a shield. Class kits go into your bags: Rogue 2 daggers; Warrior and Paladin a two-hander, a one-hander and a shield; Hunter a bow or gun; Shaman a two-hander and two different one-handers. Strength, agility and tank builds get a bow or gun, and intellect builds get a wand.
  • Ammo: every Hero gets 1000 arrows and 1000 bullets close to their level, plus a quiver and ammo pouch. Ammo is unlimited, so the stack never runs out.
  • Teleporter: click the teleporter button on the minimap (drag it around the edge to move it), or type /tp. The Dungeons page lists all 55 dungeon entrances with their level ranges (green means it fits your level, and the “For my level” tab filters to those), and it takes you just outside the door you pick. Cities, zones and raid entrances are there too. You have to be alive, out of combat, not in a dungeon, raid, battleground or arena, and not on a flight path. There's a 30-second cooldown.
  • Dungeon revive: if you die inside a dungeon or raid, you're brought back 5 seconds later with 50% health and mana at the spot where you entered. If you have the Second Chance boon, it triggers first.
  • Death Knight runes refill properly for Heroes, and Heroes get the combo point frame.
  • Tracker window: a small window you can drag lists your active boons, curses, pacts, trials and the dungeon affixes active where you are. Hover a line for details; -/+ collapses it. /gauntlet hide toggles it, /gauntlet reset ui moves it back, and /gauntlet opens the full list.
Mobile Casting needs the current launcher. The Mobile Casting boon lets you cast and channel while moving. The server allows it, but your game client also has to. The current Windows and Linux launchers (download them from the main page) build and start a patched Wow_cwm.exe next to Wow.exe. If you start the stock Wow.exe, the boon still works on the server, but your client stops the cast. On Linux and Steam, point the shortcut at the launcher (launch-classless.sh --sync, which now updates itself) or run steam/install-steam-shortcut.sh. The macOS launcher doesn't support it. A working install writes cwm.log saying “installed 4/4 hooks”.

The Boons & Curses draft

Step 7 of creation has two independent sliders. Each one goes from 0 to 8, and each step on a slider adds one row of 3 server-rolled cards. You keep one card per row. All boons and no curses is allowed, so is all curses and no boons, and so is none of either.

Boons: 3

Curses: 2

  • Re-rolls: you get 1 free row re-roll, plus 1 more for every curse-row pick. Pacts, Trials and Dungeon Affixes come from curse rows, so they use the Curses slider and earn re-rolls.
  • Conflicts: two picks clash when they push the same thing in opposite directions (Swift vs Powerful, Speedy vs Leaden, Blessed vs Mortal Wounds). Clashing cards are dimmed. Picks that push the same way stack, up to a cap.
  • Damage floors: curse damage (Blood Curse, Living Bomb, Germaphobic) never takes you below 10% health, and affix damage never kills you. The Immortal Snail Problem is the one exception: it kills on contact.
  • Dungeon affixes are shared: a dungeon gets the affix curses of every Hero inside it, and each affix counts once even if two Heroes picked it. They apply to everyone in that instance, including other players and bots.
  • Area warnings: area curses and affixes draw a ring on the ground at their real radius: Volcanic 3 yd, Entangling 4 yd, Sanguine pools 5 yd, Quaking 8 yd, Living Bomb 10 yd.
  • Seasonal affixes: each dungeon affix is a Mythic+ affix, and its card shows the season it comes from. Fortified and Tyrannical are from Legion 7.0 and have been in every season since. The newest five (Sanguine, Explosive, Spiteful, Storming and Entangling) come from seasons between Legion and Dragonflight.
  • Visuals: curses show on your character (and affixes on enemies). Boons have no glow.
